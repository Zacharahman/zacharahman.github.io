**Coarse-Grained Event Tree Analysis of GLIF Neuronal Networks**
Department of Mathematics, University of South Carolina. Advisor: Dr. Yi Sun, May 2024 – May 2025.

Generalized leaky integrate-and-fire (GLIF) networks sit between the tractability of LIF models and the biophysical detail of Hodgkin–Huxley dynamics. This work asks how much of a network's computational behavior can be recovered from the coarse structure of its spiking events alone.

### Event tree framework

- Built a Python **event tree analysis (ETA)** framework for GLIF networks to quantify network dynamics and input discriminability.
- Used conditional probability distributions over event chains to measure sensitivity to small input perturbations.

### Result

Synaptic conductance and Gaussian noise were shown to **critically shape excitability thresholds**, and therefore the network's capacity to discriminate nearby inputs.

### Earlier work

This followed an earlier project (Feb 2023 – May 2024) implementing the GLIF model itself and a simulation pipeline for neuronal responses under variable noise and synaptic coupling, supported by the **UREP Grant** and a **Magellan Fellowship**.
